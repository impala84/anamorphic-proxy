01

Why I built it

Proxy workflows shouldn’t be this fragile.

High-resolution anamorphic originals are beautiful, but unnecessarily heavy for editing. The tools I tried either kept footage squeezed, lost timecode, created a black first frame, or exposed a wall of settings.

DeProxy packages the FFmpeg workflow I could trust into one focused Mac utility. It produces compact, correctly de-squeezed proxies that retain the information needed to link back to the originals.

Square-pixel output

Width is calculated from source aspect ratio, squeeze and target height, then rounded to a valid even dimension.

(6960 ÷ 4640) × 1.5 × 1080 = 2430

Timecode kept intact

Source timecode is preserved whenever FFprobe exposes it, helping your editor match proxies back to camera originals.
